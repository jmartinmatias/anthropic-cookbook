# Elias Thys opened the Waremme-Maaseik route

The first documented player to move from VBC Waremme to VC Maaseik is **Elias Thys**, a middle blocker born in 1998. Maaseik signed him from Waremme in **May 2018** for the 2018-19 season ([Volleybalkrant](https://www.volleybalkrant.be/nieuws/2018-05-03/kersvers-landskampioen-maaseik-slaat-al-meteen-toe-op-transfermarkt)). After him the research found **six confirmed Waremme-to-Maaseik movers**. Five moved directly: Thys (2018-19), Martin Perin (2021-22), Pierre Perin (2024-25), Sam Fafchamps (2024-25) and Oskar Von Sydow (2025-26). One moved indirectly: Rasmus Breuning Nielsen played for Waremme in 2015-16 and reached Maaseik in 2019-20 after two Italian clubs. Two more names are unconfirmed. Yoran Raymaekers appears in a 2017-18 transfer listing whose direction is unclear. Antti Ronkainen appears in one snippet that almost certainly misattributes another player's history. No move effective in 2015-16 or earlier was found, and no new Waremme-to-Maaseik signing was found for 2026-27. The route also runs the other way. Kevin Klinkenberg, Eliot De Vleeschhauwer and two of the six movers (Thys and Von Sydow) went from Maaseik to Waremme, and Martin Perin returned as a coach. One limit applies to everything in this report. **None of the primary sources could be opened.** Volleybox, Wikipedia, L'Avenir, La DH, Het Belang van Limburg, CEV and Maaseik's own site returned DNS failures or proxy 403 blocks. Every fact here therefore comes from search-engine snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from full articles. The facts are most reliable where they appear in article headlines, which are visible in the URLs.

**Thys is the anchor.** Volleybalkrant reported on 3 May 2018 that Maaseik, the newly crowned champion, had signed the 19-year-old from Borgworm (the Dutch name for Waremme). He had just finished second in the Rookie of the Year vote ([Volleybalkrant](https://www.volleybalkrant.be/nieuws/2018-05-03/kersvers-landskampioen-maaseik-slaat-al-meteen-toe-op-transfermarkt)). Volleybox lists him at VBC Waremme in 2017-18 and at Maaseik from 2018-19 to 2023-24 ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/elias-thys-p11536/clubs)). In June 2019, La DH described the "ancien joueur de Waremme" winning the Belgian title in his first season at Maaseik, aged 20 ([La DH](https://www.dhnet.be/sports/sport-regional/liege/2019/06/14/elias-thys-a-ete-champion-de-belgique-en-volley-mais-la-saison-na-pas-ete-facile-SZ33CUQVSJENBJ2RL6UGGOVHMQ/)). All three tracks noted the same conflict. A March 2021 L'Avenir piece says Thys left the Hesbaye "il y a deux ans", which points to 2019 ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/waremme/2021/03/29/martin-perin-quitte-le-vbc-waremme-et-rejoint-maaseik-ZTVQPLAW2NGWLLIUCHH5PGAJYU/)). That wording is loose journalistic counting. The dated signing article, the 2019 title and the Volleybox record all fix the move at **summer 2018**.

**Martin Perin** is the best-documented move. On 29 March 2021 L'Avenir reported that the 18-year-old libero was leaving VBC Waremme for Maaseik on a **three-year contract**, while his father was Waremme's president ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/waremme/2021/03/29/martin-perin-quitte-le-vbc-waremme-et-rejoint-maaseik-ZTVQPLAW2NGWLLIUCHH5PGAJYU/); [La DH](https://www.dhnet.be/sports/sport-regional/liege/2021/03/29/volley-ball-martin-perin-waremme-fait-le-grand-saut-vers-masseik-HY4HBWC7TNHV5GPELAMAKQJZN4/)). Volleybox's Waremme B roster gives his birth year as 2002 ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/waremme-volley-b-t12314/players)). His playing career ended after a **second cardiac arrest in February 2024**, suffered during a Maaseik match played at Waremme ([RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/apres-un-2e-arret-cardiaque-sur-le-terrain-martin-perin-21-ans-met-un-terme-a-sa-carriere-de-volleyeur-professionnel-11336495)). His younger brother **Pierre** followed in 2024. L'Avenir's headline of 28 March 2024 reads "Pierre Perin quittera Waremme Volley pour Maaseik" ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2024/03/28/volley-ball-ligue-a-pierre-perin-quittera-waremme-volley-pour-maaseik-M5ILEQGVVZCEFECLV3VCTZKA3Y/)), and a 2024-25 transfer list records the same move ([Volleytimes](https://volleytimes.com/volleyball-transfers-2024-25-belgium-the-netherlands-luxembourg/)). Pierre won the 2025-26 title with Maaseik and then left for **Vero Volley Monza** in June 2026 ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2026/06/05/la-destination-de-pierre-perin-est-connue-il-jouera-en-italie-au-vero-volley-a-monza-une-opportunite-a-laquelle-je-ne-pouvais-pas-refuser-NEJNGFRM4RDFTNGYQL4YX7CBWQ/)).

**Oskar Von Sydow** is a direct move, but not a Waremme product. Waremme brought in the Swedish setter for 2024-25 ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2024/04/04/volley-ball-ligue-un-passeur-suedois-arrivera-a-waremme-volley-en-2024-2025-G5APSMUNEJD3PLEXUKB7DOGQ2Y/)). Maaseik announced him in an item titled "Oskar Von Sydow komt aan de pas" ([VC Greenyard Maaseik](https://www.vcgreenyardmaaseik.be/nieuws/oskar-von-sydow-komt-aan-de-pas/)). He went back to Waremme for 2026-27 ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2026/03/26/volley-ball-ligue-a-oskar-von-sydow-de-retour-a-waremme-volley-4OU26HR3WZGGTBW24WJNSKEEUU/)). The Volleybox record shows Waremme 2024-25, Maaseik 2025-26 and Waremme 2026-27 ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/oskar-von-sydow-p15578)). His rating stays at moderate-high only because the date of the Maaseik announcement was not visible.

## Fafchamps and Nielsen were where the tracks disagreed

**Sam Fafchamps** got three different verdicts. The early-era track rated him weak because it relied on a single summary of a March 2026 L'Avenir piece that called him an "ex de la maison" ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2026/03/31/nouvelle-distinction-pour-waremme-volley-pierre-perin-et-guilhem-hubert-nomines-pour-les-awards-de-la-saison-2025-2026-N4ICBGYCOJC5HECZZT5Y2ZHNUA/)). The cross-check track found no Maaseik link for him, but says plainly that it did not check his club page. That is an absence of evidence, not counter-evidence. The recent-era track found four separate pieces of support. Volleybox lists him on Maaseik's roster for 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27, with Welkenraedt and Waremme as earlier clubs ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/sam-fafchamps-p326142)). French Wikipedia's Maaseik roster gives **Waremme Volley as his previous club** ([Wikipedia FR](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volleybalclub_Maaseik)). A March 2025 report places him on Maaseik's side against Waremme ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2025/03/09/volley-ball-waremme-soffre-une-victoire-de-prestige-face-a-maaseik-WBOMTCIVOZBSNE76DZCADNTREM/)). And a May 2026 L'Avenir article, whose headline reads "champion de Belgique avec Maaseik, Sam Fafchamps…", dates his arrival to the start of 2024-25 and quotes him crediting Waremme ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2026/05/14/volley-ball-champion-de-belgique-avec-maaseik-sam-fafchamps-a-fait-la-fierte-de-tous-ses-proches-cest-eux-qui-me-donnent-cette-energie-durant-toute-lannee-CSTWIM7SAJEGZLGL44QMP2RKLU/)). The recent-era track's evidence is more specific, and it comes from independent sources, so it wins: **Fafchamps joined Maaseik from Waremme in 2024-25**. One question remains open: whether his Welkenraedt spell came before or after Waremme. The snippets never showed the order. Wikipedia names Waremme as his previous club, so "direct" is the better reading, which is why the table rates him moderate rather than high.

The Ronkainen lead helps settle that question. The cross-check found a snippet of Maaseik's "Profs" page listing Antti Ronkainen's previous clubs as "VBC St-Joseph Welkenraedt en Waremme Volley" ([VC Greenyard Maaseik](https://www.vcgreenyardmaaseik.be/ons-team/)). That is **exactly Fafchamps' club pair**. Volleybox, meanwhile, places Ronkainen at Plessis-Robinson in France immediately before Maaseik ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/antti-ronkainen-p24480/clubs)). The likeliest explanation is that the search tool attached Fafchamps' text to his teammate's entry on the same squad page. That would rule Ronkainen out, and it would add a second source naming Waremme last in Fafchamps' history. This is an inference from snippets, not a verified reading of the page.

**Rasmus Breuning Nielsen** appears only in the cross-check track. The recent-era track reported no indirect moves, and the early-era track looked only at transfers completed by 2015-16, not at players who passed through Waremme then. The cross-check's evidence is solid. Volleybox and the Italian league's own register both trace the Danish opposite from **VBC Waremme in 2015-16** to Revivre Milano (2016-17), then Sora (2017-18 and 2018-19), then **Maaseik in 2019-20** ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/rasmus-breuning-nielsen-p2592/clubs); [Lega Volley](https://www.legavolley.it/player/NIE-RAS-94)). One dissent comes from Global Sports Archive, which has Argos Volley instead of Sora for 2017-2019 ([GSA](https://globalsportsarchive.com/people/volleyball/rasmus-nielsen/3018416/)). That changes only the middle of his path, not the Waremme start or the Maaseik finish. His birth year of 1994 is inferred from the Lega Volley player code. He belongs in the "indirect" category: a foreign import who used Waremme as his entry into Belgium, then reached Maaseik three seasons and two clubs later.

Nielsen also resolves part of a loose end in the recent-era track. That track confirmed only Thys as an ex-Waremme player on Maaseik's side in the 8 December 2019 league match, which Maaseik won 1-3 ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/vbc-waremme-vc-greenyard-maaseik-c28008)). The cross-check ties Nielsen to the same match. So Maaseik's 2019-20 squad had **two former Waremme players, Thys and Nielsen**, although the snippet of the match page did not itself list Nielsen. Volleybox records the venue as Waremme's arena, not Maaseik.

## Nothing documented before 2018 makes Thys the first

The early-era track searched French and Dutch press for moves between 2010 and 2016 and found none. It also ruled out the two obvious candidates. **Kevin Klinkenberg** went from Mortroux to Maaseik around 2011 without playing for Waremme, and joined Waremme only as a veteran in 2023 ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2023/04/22/volley-ball-lotto-volley-leaguewaremme-volley-soffre-la-star-belge-kevin-klinkenberg-LUBNUXABCFG6NDTE7KGGLQXNEE/)). **Sébastien Dumont** joined Maaseik in 2014 from Guibertin ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/brabantwallon/mont-saint-guibert/2014/05/22/dumont-quitte-guibertin-pour-maaseik-H742BBO5MJHBTBLTIUIBLNJULQ/)). No source calls any player the "first" Waremme player at Maaseik. The closest is the 2021 coverage of Martin Perin, which said Belgium's most-titled club would then have "deux joueurs de l'arrondissement", Thys and Perin ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/waremme/2021/03/29/martin-perin-quitte-le-vbc-waremme-et-rejoint-maaseik-ZTVQPLAW2NGWLLIUCHH5PGAJYU/)). That framing treats Thys as the precursor, not as one of a long line.

Only one name could still come before Thys. **Yoran Raymaekers**, a setter from Paal who was "discovered" by Borgworm ([Paalonline](https://www.paalonline.be/cms55/nieuws/dorpsenverenigingsnieuws/item/1818-yoran-raymaekers-naar-hoogste-volleybalafdeling)), appears in Volleybox's 2017-18 transfer list beside both Waremme and Noliko Maaseik, but the direction of the move is not visible ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/transfers/2017-18/ALL/79)). The summary of his profile shows Waremme in 2016-17 and Genk in 2018-19, with no Maaseik season ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/nl/yoran-raymaekers-p4120/clubs)). The likelier reading is that he went from Maaseik's youth ranks to Waremme. Even if he did go to Maaseik in 2017, nothing shows he played for its first team. Thys therefore stands as **the first confirmed mover, both by transfer date and by arrival**. Nielsen's Waremme season (2015-16) is earlier than Thys's (2017-18), but Nielsen reached Maaseik a year after Thys did. There is one caveat. Volleybox's Waremme first-team rosters appear to be filled in only from about 2021-22, so a pre-2016 mover who never made the press cannot be ruled out ([Volleybox](https://volleybox.net/waremme-volley-t1943/players)).

Martin Perin's staff role clears up a misreading in the recent-era track. That track guessed he was Maaseik staff when a March 2025 headline had him "facing" his brother Pierre in the play-offs ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2025/03/07/volley-ball-martin-perin-affronte-son-frangin-pierre-en-challenge-play-off-cest-du-5050-2MG2IXBJQZFMZGSQAFYGPN2GQY/)). The April 2024 announcement makes the opposite reading consistent: Martin was on Waremme's bench and Pierre was playing for Maaseik. Summer 2024 was the busiest window in either direction. Pierre Perin and Fafchamps went to Maaseik, while Thys and the newly retired Martin Perin went back to Waremme.

## Conclusion

This is a recent link between the two clubs, not an old feeder line. Every confirmed move falls between 2018 and 2025, and four of the six movers arrived in the last three windows. The movers are of two kinds. The first is home-grown Hesbaye talent: Thys, the Perin brothers and probably Fafchamps, who left Waremme to win titles at Maaseik. The second is foreign imports such as Nielsen and Von Sydow, for whom Waremme was a way into Belgian volleyball. The return moves of Thys, Von Sydow and Martin Perin, and Waremme's 2026 signing of the young De Vleeschhauwer from Maaseik, suggest the two clubs now treat each other as partners for player development rather than simply as a buyer and a seller.

Three checks would change the confidence levels, and all three need direct access to Volleybox: Waremme's first-team rosters from 1999 to 2021, the order of Fafchamps' two earlier clubs, and the direction of Raymaekers' 2017-18 entry. Until someone can read those pages in full, Thys's place as the first mover is the best-supported answer, but not a closed one.
