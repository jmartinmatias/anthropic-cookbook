## Year-by-year list: direct Waremme → Maaseik moves (2016-17 to 2026-27)

### Takeaway
Five direct moves were found. Elias Thys (2018-19), Martin Perin (2021-22), Pierre Perin (2024-25), Sam Fafchamps (2024-25) and Oskar Von Sydow (2025-26). No Waremme → Maaseik move was found for 2016-17, 2017-18, 2019-20, 2020-21, 2022-23, 2023-24 or 2026-27. Thys and Von Sydow later went back to Waremme. Martin Perin retired after a second cardiac arrest in February 2024. Pierre Perin left for Vero Volley Monza (Italy) in June 2026. Fafchamps is still at Maaseik for 2026-27.
