## Which players trained or played at VBC Waremme and joined Maaseik before 2016?

### Takeaway
I found no documented Waremme-to-Maaseik transfer effective in 2015-16 or earlier. The earliest well-sourced direct move is middle blocker Elias Thys (born 1998). He played at VBC Waremme in 2017/18 and joined Maaseik for 2018/19. In other words, every documented case falls after this research window closes.

### Cited Findings
- **VBC Waremme was formed in 1999** by merging Schbing Waremme and Muronday's Oreye. It was promoted to Ligue A after finishing 2nd in 1st National Division in 2009-10 (search snippet of English Wikipedia) — [VBC Waremme, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VBC_Waremme)
- **The club name was Noliko Maaseik from 1985/86 to 2017/18 and VC Greenyard Maaseik from 2018/19** (Volleybox, via snippet) — [Volleybox transfers 2017/18](https://volleybox.net/transfers/2017-18/ALL/79); [VC Maaseik, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VC_Maaseik)
- **Waremme played its 17th Liga season in 2026-27**, which is consistent with a top-flight debut in 2010-11 (snippet) — [L'Avenir, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2026/10/16/waremme-volley-debute-sa-17e-saison-en-liga-avec-la-venue-de-maaseik-le-bon-moment-pour-les-affronter-IWNWJBXOKNALLEDM7TFBCZURSU/)

### Inferences
- If Raymaekers was at Waremme in 2016/17 and appears in the 2017/18 transfer list, any move to Maaseik would be summer 2017. That is still after 2015-16, and he may instead have gone from Maaseik's youth system to Waremme.
- The 2021 press framing points the same way (see below). "Two players from the district" at Maaseik, counting Thys and Martin Perin, suggests that before Thys there was no established line of Waremme players at Maaseik. That is circumstantial, not proof.

## Does any source describe a player as the first Waremme product to join Maaseik?

### Takeaway
No source I found uses the word "first" ("premier") for any Waremme-to-Maaseik player. The nearest is the 2021 coverage of Martin Perin. It says Belgium's most-decorated club would then have "deux joueurs de l'arrondissement" (Thys and Perin), which implicitly treats Thys as the earlier pioneer.

### Cited Findings
- 2021 coverage of Martin Perin's move says the most-titled club in Belgium would count "two players from the district" the following season, alongside Elias Thys (search summary of L'Avenir and La DH coverage) — [L'Avenir, 29 Mar 2021](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/waremme/2021/03/29/martin-perin-quitte-le-vbc-waremme-et-rejoint-maaseik-ZTVQPLAW2NGWLLIUCHH5PGAJYU/); [L'Avenir interview, 30 Mar 2021](https://www.lavenir.net/sport-local-best-of/2021/03/30/interview-martin-perin-maaseik-le-reve-3YNB66DJ7JBPLJJ23JRTZJFYXA/)
- In 2026 the Waremme side said that Waremme and Maaseik have had "des liens très étroits" for years (snippet, March 2026, in the context of Eliot De Vleeschhauwer moving the other way, from Maaseik to Waremme) — [L'Avenir, 17 Mar 2026](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/huy-waremme/sports/2026/03/17/volley-waremme-soffre-eliot-de-vleeschhauwer-un-jeune-opposite-de-maaseik-JX6A74IAQZB5RMEQD5RVGVCSJE/)
- The 2019 La DH piece on Thys calls him an "ancien joueur de Waremme" in his "première saison" at Maaseik. "First" there refers to his own first season, not to being the first Waremme player at the club — [La DH, 14 Jun 2019](https://www.dhnet.be/sports/sport-regional/liege/2019/06/14/elias-thys-a-ete-champion-de-belgique-en-volley-mais-la-saison-na-pas-ete-facile-SZ33CUQVSJENBJ2RL6UGGOVHMQ/)

### Inferences
- On current evidence, Elias Thys (2018/19) is the best-supported answer to "who was first", with Yoran Raymaekers (2017/18) as an unresolved possibility. Neither falls in or before 2015-16.

### Gaps
