## Which players on any Maaseik roster have Waremme in their club history?

### Takeaway
Five players are confirmed with Waremme before Maaseik in their club history: Elias Thys (2018/19), Rasmus Breuning Nielsen (2019/20, indirect), Martin Perin (2021/22), Pierre Perin (2024/25) and Oskar Von Sydow (2025/26). Four of the five moved directly. The Perin brothers are the only two with Waremme youth roots that are documented. A sixth name, Antti Ronkainen, comes from one weak search extract and conflicts with Volleybox, so it is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **Elias Thys:** Belgian middle blocker, born 1998. Volleybox lists VBC Waremme for 2017/18, VC Greenyard Maaseik for 2018/19-2023/24, and Waremme Volley again for 2024/25-2025/26. — [Volleybox: Elias Thys clubs](https://volleybox.net/elias-thys-p11536/clubs)
- **Elias Thys:** A June 2019 DH article says his first professional season at Maaseik ended with the Belgian title, and that he chose to stay at Maaseik for the next season. This fits a 2018/19 arrival. — [DH, 14 Jun 2019](https://www.dhnet.be/sports/sport-regional/liege/2019/06/14/elias-thys-a-ete-champion-de-belgique-en-volley-mais-la-saison-na-pas-ete-facile-SZ33CUQVSJENBJ2RL6UGGOVHMQ/)
- **Elias Thys:** A March 2021 L'Avenir article about Martin Perin compares Perin's case to Thys, who "had also left the Hesbaye for the Limburgers two years ago". That implies about 2019, not 2018. ### Inferences
- Rasmus Breuning Nielsen's birth year of 1994 is inferred from the Lega Volley player code "NIE-RAS-94". It is not stated explicitly in the sources captured.
- The Ronkainen "Waremme" previous-club text is most likely an error, either on the club page or in the search tool's summary. The source said "Welkenraedt and Waremme", and those are both French-speaking Liège-province clubs that do not appear in his documented path. Treat it as unverified until someone sees the page directly.
- The Waremme-to-Maaseik route only shows up in the sources from 2015/16. Every documented case is 2018/19 or later, apart from Nielsen's 2015/16 Waremme season. This may partly reflect Volleybox's thin Waremme rosters before about 2021, rather than a real absence of earlier transfers.
- Three of the five players (Thys, Martin Perin, Pierre Perin) are Belgian players from Waremme or the Hesbaye region. Two (Nielsen, Von Sydow) are foreign imports for whom Waremme was a step into the Belgian league. Only the Perin brothers are documented as products of Waremme's own youth system.

### Gaps
- The full season-by-season Maaseik rosters for 2005-06 to 2026-27 could not be read, because Volleybox, Wikipedia and the Maaseik site were all blocked or unreachable. Earlier players (2005-2015) with a Waremme youth background therefore cannot be ruled out.
- Elias Thys's youth club before VBC Waremme 2017/18 was not found. Whether he was a Waremme youth product or joined from another club in 2017 is unknown.
- Oskar Von Sydow's birth year was not found.
- Elias Thys's 2018/19 arrival rests on Volleybox and the DH article. The L'Avenir "two years ago" (2021) wording implies 2019, a small inconsistency.
- No CEV competition roster pages for Maaseik's European seasons were retrieved, so they could not be used as a check.

## Which Waremme roster players later played for Maaseik?

### Takeaway
The reverse check only worked partly, because Waremme's Volleybox roster pages could not be opened. Search extracts suggest the first-team rosters are only filled in from about 2021/22-2022/23. Starting from Waremme players, the same five names come up: Nielsen (Waremme 2015/16), Thys (2017/18), Martin Perin (2018/19-2020/21), Pierre Perin (2021/22-2023/24) and Von Sydow (2024/25). No other Waremme roster player was found to have later played for Maaseik.

### Cited Findings
- VBC Waremme was founded in 1999 from the merger of Schbing Waremme and Muronday's Oreye. It was called VBC Waremme from 1999/00 to 2020/21 and Waremme Volley from 2021/22. — [Volleybox: Waremme Volley](https://volleybox.net/waremme-volley-t1943); [Wikipedia: VBC Waremme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VBC_Waremme)
- A search extract said Volleybox's Waremme first-team rosters run from about 2021/22-2022/23 (2025/26 includes Tijl Van Looveren and Guilhem Hubert). It did not find earlier first-team rosters. The Waremme Volley B page for 2016/17-2017/18 lists only Martin Perin. — [Volleybox: Waremme Volley rosters](https://volleybox.net/waremme-volley-t1943/players); [Volleybox: Waremme Volley B](https://volleybox.net/waremme-volley-b-t12314/players)
- Former Waremme players named in sources: Thierry Courtois (2003-2006, a former top-flight player) and Philippe Barca-Cysique (2016-2018), per French Wikipedia. Kevin Klinkenberg, Kalle Madsen and Sam Fafchamps also appear on Volleybox. No Maaseik spell after Waremme was found for any of them. — [fr.wikipedia: Waremme Volley](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waremme_Volley); [Volleybox: Waremme Volley rosters](https://volleybox.net/waremme-volley-t1943/players)
- Nielsen played for VBC Waremme in the 2015/16 Belgian Liga A and Belgian Cup. He then faced Waremme as a Maaseik player in a December 2019 league match. — [Volleybox: Rasmus Breuning Nielsen](https://volleybox.net/rasmus-breuning-nielsen-p2592); [Volleybox match: VBC Waremme vs Maaseik 2019/20](https://volleybox.net/vbc-waremme-vc-greenyard-maaseik-c28008)

### Inferences
- Thierry Courtois, Barca-Cysique, Madsen and Fafchamps showed no Maaseik link in any search extract. This is weak negative evidence, because their Volleybox clubs pages were not checked one by one.

### Gaps
- Waremme first-team rosters for 1999/00 to about 2020/21 could not be enumerated. Players from those years who later joined Maaseik are only known where press or player-page searches surfaced them.
- Sam Fafchamps, Kalle Madsen, Vianney Van Dooren, Roman Abinet and other recent Waremme players were not checked one by one for later Maaseik spells.

## Known leads (Elias Thys, Martin Perin, Pierre Perin) and any others

All three leads are confirmed as direct moves from Waremme to Maaseik: Thys in 2018/19, Martin Perin in 2021/22 and Pierre Perin in 2024/25.
